# Demo script: the STREAM burn

Written for: viewers of the X video, people who know crypto but have not read the thread. It is also your run sheet for the recording.

Target 60 seconds, voice over a 1440x900 screen record of the deployed page. Every fact below comes from `CONTENT.md`. `VOICEOVER.md` is the shorter shot list this expands.

## Before you hit record

- Open the Vercel URL in a fresh window at 1440x900, scrolled to the top. Hard refresh so the load burn is ready.
- Hide bookmarks and extensions. Browser zoom 100%.
- Know that the hero burn plays by itself about half a second after load, then rests on the burned state. To start the slider from full supply, click **Before**.
- Rehearse the hover on the receipt signature. It is the last proof shot.
- Do not mention the bounty. Do not say "my app." Do not say the burn moved the price. Do not say the xSTREAM swap is live.

## Script

Each beat has the **Do** steps in order and the **Say** lines to speak while you do them. Start talking on the first step of each beat.

### Beat 1 · 0:00–0:05 · The hook

**Do**
1. Page loads at the top. The dots burn left to right on their own.
2. Keep the cursor still. Hold on the headline.

**Say**
> They did not lock the treasury. They deleted 70% of supply.

### Beat 2 · 0:05–0:18 · What burned

**Do**
1. Click **Before**. The dots go all green and the card reads `1,000M supply`.
2. Click and hold the slider thumb. Drag slowly from left to right over about 6 seconds.
3. Stop at the right end. Rest on `1,000M → 300M` and `699.99M gone`.

**Say**
> Streamflow Foundation burned every STREAM it held. That is 70% of total supply, 699.99 million tokens, in one transaction. Supply went from 1 billion to 300 million. Each dot here is a million tokens.

### Beat 3 · 0:18–0:30 · Why a burn, not a lock

**Do**
1. Scroll down once so the three cards fill the screen.
2. Click **Hold**. Wait one beat while the cells drift away.
3. Click **Lock**. Wait one beat while the outlines unlock.
4. Click **Burn**. Let the cells glow and go out.

**Say**
> Why burn? Holding means the market still has to price tokens that can be sold. *(on Hold)*
> A lock still ends, and a vest still unlocks. *(on Lock)*
> A burn does not come back. And there is no mint authority, so the supply is capped at 300 million for good. *(on Burn)*

### Beat 4 · 0:30–0:40 · What is left

**Do**
1. Scroll down once so the four rows and the strip above them are in frame.
2. Move the cursor onto the first row, **Freely circulating**. The strip highlights its segment.
3. Move down to the second row, **Investor and early-contributor vesting**.
4. Move the cursor off the rows.

**Say**
> The founder also returned about 90% of his own allocation. Part was burned, part went back to the team bucket. *(cursor still)*
> What is left is the free float, investor vesting, a team reserve, and staking rewards. *(as you hover the rows)*

### Beat 5 · 0:40–0:52 · The proof

**Do**
1. Scroll down once to the receipt. Let the lines and the checkmarks draw in.
2. Move the cursor to the signature, `4rbViHbm…hnchZNv`. Hover and rest there.

**Say**
> Existing products did not change: locks, vesting, staking, airdrops, payouts. Same mint. *(while the receipt draws in)*
> And you do not have to trust this page. The proof is the burn transaction. A real burn instruction, not a transfer to a dead wallet. *(while hovering the signature)*

### Beat 6 · 0:52–1:00 · Close

**Do**
1. Scroll to the bottom so the footer links Transaction, Foundation and Blog are in frame.
2. Keep the cursor still. Do not click.

**Say**
> Everything is on chain. The transaction, the Foundation and @streamflow_fi are linked in the post.

About 150 spoken words. If you run long, cut the sentence about the dots and the sentence about the free float.
